# DOHNUT Visual AI Engine™

## Objective

Menukar creative brief berstruktur kepada production-ready visual prompt untuk Google Flow dan visual AI lain sambil mengekalkan DOHNUT Brand Truth.

## Architecture

```text
BRIEF
 ↓
CREATIVE GENOME
 ↓
DOH LANGUAGE + DOH CINEMA + DOH BOY
 ↓
POP CULTURE ADAPTER
 ↓
PROMPT COMPILER
 ↓
GOOGLE FLOW / IMAGE / VIDEO AI

VISUAL QA
 ↓
ASSET REGISTRY
 ↓

```

## Visual Tiers

| Tier | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Product-only hero |
| 2 | Brand visual |
| 3 | Campaign scene |
| 4 | Pop-culture transformation |
| 5 | Full hero campaign |

## Master Visual Principles

- Premium commercial food photography.
- Tactile, rounded, soft and slightly squishy materiality.
- Realistic dough and filling behaviour.
- Bold, clean brand-colour environments.
- Controlled studio or cinematic lighting.
- Product remains the hero.
- Avoid plastic-looking food and generic stock-photo aesthetics.

## Visual Quality Gate

Every output must pass:

### Can I Taste It?

Dough, filling, glaze and texture look appetizing.

### Can I Feel It?

The material appears tactile, soft and dimensional.

### Can I Recognize It?

The composition feels unmistakably DOHNUT even without relying on the logo.
